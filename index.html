Our story

Velvet Bean started with one grinder, one small counter and a simple idea: coffee should feel as good as it tastes. We roast in small batches, bake every morning and keep the lights low enough for long conversations.

Whether you are studying, catching up with friends or just need a quiet cup, there is always a seat for you.
